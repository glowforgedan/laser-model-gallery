A NOTE ON PERSPECTIVE

Made to explore.
Honestly reconstructed.

These are authored appearance models, reconstructed from manuals, photographs and footage. They preserve useful component detail and distinct head configurations.

They are not manufacturer CAD or measurement-verified hardware. Dimensions describe the models, never a verified machine envelope or work area. Individual notes keep inferred details and provisional identities visible.

Only our authored models and renders are shown. Research photographs and videos are not republished.
